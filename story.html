    The first visit went well, they needed a bit more. Pedro was meant to go to Toulouse, just when the pandemic arrived. From there, countless hours of skyping helped them (especially Pedro) to stay sane.

    More talking, more visits, meeting each other's family… Until Jimi officially moved in with Pedro in the UK. Mardle Street, there we go.

    June 2026

  • Duolingo help us

    July 2026

    Their lives are a train that never stops. Engagement done, next stop: Budapest. Jimedro moved for a six-month period to Budapest, just in time to celebrate a well-deserved LGBTQ Pride after a change of political winds in the country. A new adventure to come!

  • The adventure continues
