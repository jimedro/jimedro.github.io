How to get to Can Marial

✈️ By Plane

Fly into Barcelona-El Prat Airport (BCN). From there, you can rent a car, taxi, or transit.

🚂 By Train

Take the R1 commuter train line (Rodalies) from central Barcelona directly up to Pineda de Mar.

🚗 By Car

Drive north along the C-32 highway or N-II coastal road from Barcelona. Parking available on-site.
